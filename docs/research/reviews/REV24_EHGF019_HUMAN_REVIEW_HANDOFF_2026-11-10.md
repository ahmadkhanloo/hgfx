## Original first-divergence fact and proposed mechanism

- At original zero-based **trial 8**, HGF **level 3** posterior variance `infStates.sa`: MATLAB `8.209456713045242`; original HGFX 1.0.0 `NaN`. The corresponding top-level prediction `sahat=8.209456713045242` is finite on both. First observation log likelihood invalidity in HGFX occurs later, at zero-based **trial 11**. First finite binary64 nonidentity at trial 0 is not a causal divergence. See [full trace report](../REV24_EHGF019_ORIGINAL_TRACE_RESULT_2026-11-10.md).
- MATLAB's eHGF top-level correction uses `pi_j=pihat_j+max(0,correction)`; HGFX 1.0.0 used `pihat+np.maximum(0.0,correction)`. The archived mean `mu3=1009.0446083588881` and omega `1.391502762318833` imply `exp(1010.4361111212067)` overflows float64. The hypothesized `Inf*0 -> NaN` correction is **RECONSTRUCTED from archived states and source**, not directly instrumented in both runtime paths. MATLAB omits NaN in scalar `max`, while NumPy `maximum` propagates it; NumPy `fmax` omits it. This is a **likely numerical compatibility cause**, not a proven scientific/model limitation.
- The development patch changes one numerical operation **only** in the eHGF precision update, `np.maximum` to `np.fmax`. No added clipping, no seed/data/model change, no new thresholds. HGF/uHGF branches, optimizer, published Wheel and all old evidence are unchanged.
- The scalar golden `tests/unit/test_ehgf_max_nan_compat.py` uses archived scalar operands at original trial 8; successful scalar behavior is **not proof** that the *whole repaired 128-trial trajectory* or 200-member eHGF family agrees with MATLAB.
