## 4. Scientific adjudication and remaining blockers

**CRITICAL — Original fit and Level 2.** The binding historical result is **63/64 SCIENTIFIC NONPASS**. At subject index 7, `traj.mu` first exceeds tolerance at zero-based `[83,1]` and `traj.muhat` at `[84,1]` (level 2). Each has four failures, with maximum absolute difference `1.5704625655477145e-9`. The trajectory, final, negative likelihood/joint and prediction/residual tolerance pairs are `atol=3e-10, rtol=3e-8`. Hessian, LME and AIC/BIC use `2e-8, 2e-6`; covariance and correlation use `5e-8, 5e-6`; init is exact. The pointwise bound scales against the MATLAB value, which is why near-zero cells can fail while the endpoint and the other compared quantities pass. The recorded equality of 101 history slots compares allocated history length rather than observed iteration counts. The native termination experiment separately reports gradient convergence on both sides and 24 HGFX iterations with zero resets.
