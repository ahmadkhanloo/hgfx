## Independently verified finding on **all 71 first-gradient x locations**

| Original 71 sample x source-coordinate paths | Nonidentical HGFX vs MATLAB binary64 at same x | Bitwise identical |
|---|---:|---:|
| **128-element observation log-likelihood vector** | **71 / 71 nodes** | 0 / 71 |
| **Regular-trial log-likelihood sum** | **14 / 71 nodes** | 57 / 71 |
| Negative log-likelihood scalar | **14 / 71 nodes** | 57 / 71 |
| Free perceptual Gaussian prior terms and their sum | **0 / 71 nodes** | 71 / 71 |
| Free observation Gaussian prior term and its sum | **0 / 71 nodes** | 71 / 71 |
| **Complete original negative log-joint scalar** | **14 / 71 nodes** | 57 / 71 |

The **14 original source node indices** (zero-based) where `log_likelihood`, `negative_log_likelihood` and final `negative_log_joint` differ **exactly coincide**: `[3,13,14,15,24,26,28,29,30,42,43,44,51,68]`. The **Gaussian priors are not the source** of any first-gradient same-x mismatch here; original all 2 perceptual and 1 observation free prior terms/totals are bitwise equal for every 71 nodes. All 71 nodes nonetheless have **at least one different per-trial observation likelihood scalar**, although these differences produce an exactly identical final log-likelihood sum at 57 nodes. The first-stage numeric divergence is therefore already present **at or before trial observation likelihood evaluation** and cannot be explained solely by a mismatch of prior terms or by summing otherwise identical per-trial likelihood values.

**Attribution limits:** Trial-level observation log-likelihoods depend on **both** the HGF perceptual forward path and the observation response model. This source-term decomposition does not isolate which upstream arithmetic operation or function introduces a different trial likelihood, and does not distinguish whether a differing final 14-node total is due to the trial-term differences alone or also to summation order on differing trial arrays. The original HGFX `_matlab_sum` has a frozen four-lane policy validated elsewhere, but its full 128-node exact reduction parity is NOT asserted by this observation alone. An additional archive-only aggregation of which of the 128 trials differ at which original source nodes may identify the most informative next frozen function/stage; no post-hoc scientific threshold should be introduced.
