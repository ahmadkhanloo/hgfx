## Rationale and selection disclosure

The unchanged original public HGFX v1.0.0 vs frozen MATLAB 8.2.0 full-fit cohort remains **63/64 SCIENTIFIC NONPASS** (zero-based subject 7, four `traj.mu` and four `traj.muhat` violations), with original nested cohorts 15/16 and 1/1. [Subsequent **post-result selected** original private-objective stencil study](PERF01D_RIDDERS_STENCIL_ANOMALY_RESULTS_2026-11-07.md) demonstrated one/two-ULP objective differences at some identical stencil x, first `realmax` sentinel, NaN Richardson diagonals and a different late best-extrapolant selection. Those are numerical diagnostics, not causal proof of the downstream original public-fit difference.

This NEW experiment intentionally selects original history records **0** (same initial exact x for both engines) and **6** (maximum observed discrepancy among the predeclared 50), and optionally all recorded **25 HGFX-own** original history x. The selection was informed by observations and is **not a prospective gradient or public scientific acceptance gate**. The protocol is committed **before** running the new optimizer counterfactuals; preserve all arms and negative outcomes. Do NOT alter the frozen original MATLAB or HGFX source, objective, data, prior, optimizer tolerances, Ridders formulas, acceptance matrices, original evidence, benchmark eligibility or paper claims.
