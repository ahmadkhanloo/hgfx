# Cover letter — Neuroinformatics (Springer Nature)

**Article type:** Software Original Article

Dear Editors of *Neuroinformatics*,

Please consider our manuscript, **“HGFX: an open-source Python/JAX implementation for reproducible Hierarchical Gaussian Filter analyses,”** as a Software Original Article.

The Hierarchical Gaussian Filter is widely used in computational neuroscience and computational psychiatry, yet established analyses depend on numerical and workflow conventions of the MATLAB toolbox. We present HGFX 1.0.0, an MIT-licensed, publicly distributed Python/JAX implementation, and a reproducible validation study against a frozen HGF Toolbox 8.2.0 reference. The work addresses software distribution, model/configuration compatibility, fitting and simulation workflows, model-selection decisions, physical-GPU applicability in a tested scope, and a carefully mapped comparison with pyhgf. The published package can be installed from PyPI without a MATLAB runtime.

We believe the paper fits the journal's Software Original Article category because it describes a new research software product, provides instructions for installation and use, and evaluates numerical exactness with traceable evidence. In paired model-selection validation, all 36 BIC winners agree. Equally important, we retain incomplete parameter-recovery criteria, sensitive fitting cases, and inconclusive trial-horizon evidence rather than promoting them to scientific successes. We do not claim a new generative model, universal accuracy, or unmeasured acceleration.

The source, versioned release, supporting machine-readable artifacts, table/figure generators and reproducibility instructions are available at https://github.com/ahmadkhanloo/hgfx. The manuscript is original and will not be under consideration by another journal at the time of submission. All declarations and metadata will be confirmed in the submission system.

Thank you for considering this work.
