# Transfer cover letter — Neuroscience Informatics (Elsevier)

**Article type:** Original Research

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Team,

I wish to submit the transferred manuscript **“Reproducible Hierarchical Gaussian Filter workflows in Python/JAX: scoped validation against the MATLAB toolbox”** for consideration as an Original Research article in *Neuroscience Informatics*.

The original submission, JNEUMETH-D-26-01047, was declined by *Journal of Neuroscience Methods* for journal-scope reasons. Elsevier subsequently offered a manuscript transfer with guaranteed peer review. This revised version emphasizes the informatics and computational-model-validation contribution rather than presenting the work as a new neurological or clinical experiment.

HGFX 1.0.0 is a publicly available Python/JAX implementation of documented Hierarchical Gaussian Filter workflows. We assess agreement with the frozen MATLAB HGF Toolbox 8.2.0 in reference demonstrations, fitting and simulation, model-selection decisions, and selected execution backends. The validation reports 36/36 paired BIC winner agreement but also preserves parameter-recovery failures and the inconclusive trial-horizon extension. A physical-GPU result is restricted to the executed fitting cells, while the pyhgf comparison is restricted to an explicitly mapped common scope. Thus the principal advance is transparent, reproducible computational methodology, not a clinical efficacy claim or unmeasured performance superiority.

I request confirmation of the financial arrangement before final submission; this draft does not authorize any publication charge.

The manuscript is not under simultaneous consideration elsewhere. I would be pleased to provide any further information about the software and evidence package.
