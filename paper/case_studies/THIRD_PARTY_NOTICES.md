## Hess et al. (2025), SPIRL

Article: Alexander J. Hess and coauthors, *Bayesian Workflow for Generative Modeling in Computational Psychiatry*, Computational Psychiatry 9(1), 76–99, DOI https://doi.org/10.5334/cpsy.116. Article copyright The Authors 2025; CC BY 4.0, as stated at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11951975/ . A failed PDF retrieval is recorded in the manifest; do not assume that a linked paper was downloaded.

The full-text publisher HTML was subsequently retrieved from https://reference-global.com/article/10.5334/cpsy.116?tab=article and saved unchanged as `spirl_2025/paper.html`. This is a full-text HTML copy, not a publisher PDF. The HTML may reference externally hosted display assets.

Dataset: *Dataset from a speed incentivized probabilistic reward learning task*, Zenodo record https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10663643. Creator attribution and version metadata are preserved in `spirl_2025/zenodo_record.json`. All five data-deposit files are unchanged. License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This is a preprocessed public release, not the full raw study dataset.

Analysis software: Alex Hess / Translational Neuromodeling Unit, University of Zurich and ETH Zurich. Original repository https://gitlab.ethz.ch/tnu/code/hessetal_spirl_analysis ; snapshot `86fd5122a744acfb5bc7ba12720426d8b4aa0ffa`. License GPL version 3 or later, preserved in `spirl_2025/UPSTREAM_LICENSE`, original README, file headers and source archive. Archive created with `git archive`; original file contents unchanged; no submodule contents bundled. This is a separate upstream source archive, not an adaptation incorporated into the HGFX library.

## Arthur et al. (2023)

Tom Arthur, Sam Vine, Gavin Buckingham, Mark Brosnan, Mark Wilson and David Harris. *Testing predictive coding theories of autism spectrum disorder using models of active inference*. PLOS Computational Biology 19(9):e1011473. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011473 . Copyright the authors 2023. The publisher article is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution; its original PDF and notices are preserved unchanged. [Publisher license statement](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011473).

The separate OSF project https://osf.io/5k48n/ declared no node license in the checked API. Its data and MATLAB scripts are downloaded into ignored `local_only/arthur_2023/` for inspection, not publicly redistributed. Article licensing is not assumed to cover the external deposit. Public inventory records source links, sizes and source-provided checksums.

## Henco et al. (2020)

Lara Henco and coauthors. *Aberrant computational mechanisms of social learning and decision-making in schizophrenia and borderline personality disorder*. PLOS Computational Biology 16(9):e1008162. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008162 . Copyright the authors 2020. The publisher article is CC BY; its PDF is preserved unchanged. [Publisher notice and data restrictions](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008162).

The article explicitly restricts sharing of individual raw data and points to processed data/code at https://osf.io/8kfph/ . No OSF node license was declared. No clinical raw records are redistributed here. The attempted processed-data/code archive download failed in this acquisition; the source README is locally available and excluded from Git.
