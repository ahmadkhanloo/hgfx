## Upload order

1. Open the official journal portal and sign in or create an author account.
2. Start **New Submission**, select **Software Original Article** (the portal may display equivalent editorial article labels).
3. Enter title, abstract, keywords, author affiliation/email, funding/competing interests, and all required metadata.
4. Upload `HGFX_Neuroinformatics_Manuscript.docx` as the **main manuscript**; if the portal requests a separate title page, upload `HGFX_Neuroinformatics_Title_Page.docx` too.
5. Upload `HGFX_Neuroinformatics_Supplementary_Appendices.pdf` as **Supplementary Material** and the original figure files separately only if the portal requires them. Do not mix supplementary files into the article's primary text.
6. Paste/upload `HGFX_Neuroinformatics_Cover_Letter.docx` and check suggested-reviewer rules if requested.
7. Review the system-generated manuscript proof and press Submit. Record the new submission number.

## Economic route

The journal is **hybrid**. The publisher states **Subscription publication without an APC** is available after acceptance. Choose **Subscription / non-open access** for zero APC, unless an institution/funder requires open access. The posted OA APC on 2026-10-29 is EUR 3990 (or GBP 3190 / USD 5090, excluding taxes). Selection of subscription does not itself guarantee editorial acceptance.

## Scientific cautions

The paper reports 36/36 paired BIC winner agreement, but does **not** establish general parameter identifiability. Physical Tesla T4 evidence addresses only tested correctness/applicability, not throughput. The pyhgf comparison is one mapped common-scope cell; response NLL is not directly comparable. The prospective P3 trial-horizon gate is inconclusive with known model/optimizer mismatch classifications. Do not silently change these findings for readability.
