**Re: Submission of “HGFX: a validated Python/JAX reproduction of the Hierarchical Gaussian Filter toolbox” — Software Original Article**

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript for publication as a **Software Original Article** in *Neuroinformatics*. HGFX 1.0.0 is a publicly released, MIT-licensed Python/JAX toolbox designed to reproduce the documented behavior of a frozen version of the MATLAB Hierarchical Gaussian Filter (HGF) toolbox without requiring MATLAB for normal scientific use.

The manuscript's central contribution is not the claim of the first Python HGF implementation. Rather, it presents a reproducible and explicitly bounded cross-language compatibility framework for neuroscience analysis: configuration and parameter semantics, filtering and fitting behavior, official demonstration workflows, model selection, and CPU/GPU applicability are linked to versioned reference evidence and predeclared criteria. The work also includes a controlled, semantically mapped comparison with pyhgf 0.3.2 and explicitly preserves negative, inconclusive, and not-directly-comparable findings. In particular, we do not represent matching reference limitations as evidence of parameter identifiability or claim general speed advantages.

We believe this study aligns with *Neuroinformatics*' interest in neuroscience software tools and in rigorous evaluation of their accuracy, distribution, usability, and reproducibility. The complete code, released package, supporting machine-readable results, figure/table generation scripts, and replication instructions are publicly available at https://github.com/ahmadkhanloo/hgfx and https://pypi.org/project/hgfx/1.0.0/ .

This work is submitted as a software-validation study, with no new participant data. The author is the maintainer of the software and has disclosed this relationship, the use of generative AI assistance, and the limits of the validation evidence in the manuscript.

Thank you for your consideration.
