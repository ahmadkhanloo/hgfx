# Submission checklist — 2026-11-04

| Independent status | Current outcome | Remaining gate |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific evidence ready | Frozen compatibility evidence documented; full planned case-study scope NOT READY | No eligible external real-data replication; human claim verification pending |
| Editorial package ready | READY FOR AUTHOR REVIEW, not submission | Final author review, declarations and venue-specific preflight |
| Zero-fee route cleared | NO / OPEN | All mandatory charges and institutional conditions need explicit clearance |
| Author approval pending | YES | Author must verify claims, affiliation, contributions, funding, conflicts and final files |

Completed locally:

- Full English manuscript rewrite, mathematical summary, software comparison,
  workflow example, evidence-bound results/discussion, and explicit application gap.
- Pain snapshot/hash/dependency/model audit and blocked protocol record; SPIRL
  fallback audit. Original upstream code and participant data remain local.
- Independent agent scientific review; source-based corrections incorporated.
  This does not constitute human verification.
- Separate agreement/accuracy figure, workflow schematic, preserved recovery,
  pyhgf, horizon and backend figures with provenance.
- Editable Markdown, flat LaTeX source archive, BibTeX and rendered main/supplement
  PDFs. Generic author-review article layout; official template download failed.
- Draft cover letter, preprint change summary, policy dossier and claim ledger.

Not cleared or not performed:

- No actual journal submission, preprint upload, email, payment or final author
  attestation.
- No successful Pain/SPIRL fit, participant figure or paper-result reproduction.
- No new recovery, tolerance change, accelerator benchmark or frozen-result edit.
- No claim of exact historical Pain replication, universal model identifiability,
  package-wide pyhgf equivalence, clinical validity or GPU speedup.
- Complete version-of-record exemplar review is pending subscription access;
  complete author versions were read with their version differences identified.
- Exact journal indexing/metric documentation remains open; no quartile or impact
  factor claim is used in the manuscript or selection rationale.

Sending requires closing actual scientific/administrative gaps and author
approval. A package that opens correctly is not automatically submission-ready.
