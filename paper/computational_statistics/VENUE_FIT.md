# Venue fit and comparison with published software articles

Checked 2026-11-04. Computational Statistics is selected for its explicit
software/package scope, conditional on zero mandatory fees. Fit is an editorial
judgment, not a measured acceptance chance. No Q1/impact-factor assertion is made.

## BARMPy

Van Boxel, *BARMPy: Bayesian additive regression models Python package*,
[DOI 10.1007/s00180-024-01535-9](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00180-024-01535-9).
Publisher labels it Software Article, online 2024, volume 40 (2025).
The publisher page is subscription preview. The complete author version was
opened at [arXiv:2404.04738v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.04738v1), sections 2-5;
it is not silently treated as identical to the final published version.

The package contribution makes an existing modeling method accessible through
Python interfaces, documentation and scikit-learn integration. Sections 3-4 tie
interface decisions to a clumped-isotope example and error/timing comparisons.
The example's inversion requirement and computational cost are discussed as
limitations. HGFX should similarly connect its compatibility contract to actual
user decisions. Unlike BARMPy's predictive comparisons, HGFX's strongest evidence
is paired reference behavior, and its current external application is blocked.

## eks

Duong, *Statistical visualisation of tidy and geospatial data in R via kernel
smoothing methods in the eks package*,
[DOI 10.1007/s00180-024-01543-9](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00180-024-01543-9).
Publisher labels it Software Article, online 2024, volume 40 (2025).
The complete author version was opened at
[arXiv:2203.01686v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.01686v4), sections 2-7; the final
publisher full text was not accessible without subscription.

Its contribution bridges established kernel methods to tidy/geospatial workflows.
Air-quality and plant-location data illustrate interpretable outputs; section 6
shows exports to GIS software. The paper organizes capabilities around statistical
tasks rather than a module inventory. HGFX adopts that reader orientation, while
retaining failed recovery and its lack of an executed external real-data case.
This difference remains an editorial weakness, not an obstacle solved by prose.

## Decision and alternatives

Proceed with a reviewable author draft on compatibility evidence; **do not label
it submission-ready**. Novelty relative to pyhgf and applied utility remain the
main review risks. Two recent software articles support article-type fit but do
not establish that the present evidence is sufficient for acceptance. Exact
version-of-record full-text review of these exemplars remains pending access.

[JOSS eligibility](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html) was
opened. Research impact and public development history need a separate audit;
March 2027 is only a possible reassessment window from the recorded September
2026 public history. Elapsed time alone is insufficient. JSS is a possible later
route only after its own fee/rule audit. No simultaneous submission is proposed.
