# Cover letter — Journal of Neuroscience Methods

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **“HGFX: a validated Python/JAX reproduction of the Hierarchical Gaussian Filter toolbox,”** for publication as a Research Article in the *Journal of Neuroscience Methods*.

The Hierarchical Gaussian Filter (HGF) is widely used in computational neuroscience and computational psychiatry to model learning under uncertainty and volatility. HGFX provides a MATLAB-independent Python/JAX implementation designed to preserve the scientific and numerical behavior of a frozen HGF Toolbox 8.2.0 reference within explicitly documented scopes.

The manuscript is not presented as a software announcement alone. It reports a pre-specified validation study covering configuration semantics, forward trajectories, observation models, fitting and statistical surfaces, simulation workflows, official demo behavior, model-selection results, backend agreement, and a scoped comparison with pyhgf. The paper also retains failed parameter-recovery evidence and reference limitations rather than relabeling them as success. A separate prospectively frozen trial-horizon experiment is reported with its failed paired-integrity gate and is not used to support a stronger identifiability claim.

We believe the work fits the journal because it addresses reproducibility and methodological continuity for an established computational-neuroscience model, allowing existing HGF workflows to be reproduced in a contemporary Python environment while making the validation boundary explicit.

HGFX 1.0.0 is openly available on GitHub and PyPI, and the manuscript’s tables, figures, and numerical claims are tied to committed machine-readable evidence and reproducibility records. The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and no specific external funding supported this work. The author declares that he is the developer and maintainer of HGFX; this relationship is disclosed in the manuscript.

Thank you for considering this submission.
